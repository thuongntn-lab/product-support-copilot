# Product Support Copilot Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 10, 2026

Product Support Copilot is an internal ecommerce operations extension. It is
used to validate Customall clipart folders, upload approved mockup assets, read
approved Shipping and GMC values, and fill matching Shopify product fields.

## Data processed

The extension may process the current Shopify store handle, product ID, product
type, product tags, selected local filenames, upload status, and approved
product-field values returned by the team's lookup service.

## How data is used

Data is used only to perform the upload, validation, lookup, and product-editing
actions initiated by the operator. The extension does not sell data, use data
for advertising, or create user profiles.

## Data sharing

Product Type lookup requests are sent only to the approved internal lookup
service operated by the team. That service reads the two approved Lark Sheets.
No data is transferred to unrelated third parties.

For internal operational reporting, the extension may send a random installation
identifier, extension version, heartbeat timestamps, and derived active-use
seconds to the approved lookup service. The identifier is scoped to a Chrome
profile; the extension does not collect hardware serial numbers, passwords,
authentication cookies, page contents, or IP addresses for this reporting.

## Authentication and sensitive data

The extension does not collect passwords, payment information, or Shopify/Lark
authentication cookies. Lark authentication remains on the approved lookup
server. A team API key is stored locally by Chrome and is used only to restrict
access to that service.

## Local storage

The extension stores connection settings and limited upload history in Chrome
local extension storage. Operators can clear upload history from the extension
interface or remove all stored data by uninstalling the extension.

## Retention

Usage summaries are retained by the team for operational planning according to
the team's internal retention policy. Operators may clear local extension data;
that does not remove already-uploaded aggregate usage records from the server.

## Contact

Questions about this extension should be directed to the ecommerce operations
team responsible for Product Support Copilot.
